A legacy rooted in Toril.

The supplied community account remembers Agton through the places he lived, the land he farmed, and the relationships he held as southern Davao changed.

Where he lived and farmed

The account places his residence and farming activities in Bago Gallera, Balyok, Daliao, and eventually Toril Proper.

Places named in the account: Bago Gallera, Balyok, Daliao, and Toril Proper.

Leadership during change

Local histories describe him as a kind, accommodating leader who negotiated and shared land with newcomers and other communities as settlement expanded.

Remembered today

His story helps connect the Indigenous history of Toril with the ancestral presence of the Bagobo Tagabawa in southern Davao.
