## Content plan implemented

1. **Community-Based Education in Astorga** — the documented PANGANDOY partnership and its local setting.
2. **Learning Materials With Local Context** — documented contextualized materials and the reported writeshop.
3. **Folk Speech in Scholarly Documentation** — the defined scope of a USeP publication on proverbs and riddles.

The plan avoids the existing public pages’ main topics: governance, ancestral land, Mount Apo ecology/agriculture, Inabal, and general historical identity. It uses the existing shared chapter rail; no Community-specific JavaScript is required.
