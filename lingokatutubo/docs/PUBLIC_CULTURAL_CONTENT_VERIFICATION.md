# Public Cultural Content Verification Matrix

This document provides a claim-by-claim factual audit of all public cultural content across `/bagobo-tagabawa/`, `/culture/`, and `/nature/` for LingoKatutubo.

## Source Priority Ranking
1. Bagobo-Tagabawa community/cultural-bearer documentation & oral tradition records
2. National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP) — ADSDPP & CADT documentation
3. University of Southeastern Philippines (USeP) / Credible academic research
4. National Museum of the Philippines — Ethnological and textile archives
5. National Commission for Culture and the Arts (NCCA) — GAMABA & SLT documentation
6. Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) / Department of Agriculture (DA)
7. Peer-reviewed academic linguistic and anthropological sources
8. Established cultural institutions and museums

---

## Content Scope & Classification Categories
- `TAGABAWA-SPECIFIC`: Supported by sources specifically identifying the Bagobo-Tagabawa.
- `BROADER-BAGOBO`: Supported by sources describing Bagobo groups generally; labeled as broader Bagobo where appropriate.
- `GENERAL-ENVIRONMENTAL`: Supported by environmental/scientific sources (e.g., DENR ecological data).
- `GENERAL-GOVERNMENT`: Supported by official government institutional/program sources (e.g., NCIP, NCCA, DA).
- `UNCERTAIN`: Insufficiently grounded; withheld from public display.

---

## Publication Status Definitions
- `VERIFIED`: Supported by authoritative source; permitted in public UI.
- `VERIFIED_BROADER_BAGOBO`: Verified for broader Bagobo; permitted in public UI when explicitly contextualized.
- `COMMUNITY_REVIEW_NEEDED`: Sensitive or localized claim requiring direct community confirmation before public display.
- `NEEDS_RESEARCH`: Unverified claim removed from public UI until reliable documentation is identified.
- `REMOVE`: Unsubstantiated, inaccurate, or unsupported claim deleted entirely.
